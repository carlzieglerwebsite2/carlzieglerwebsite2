# Carl Ziegler — Academic and Research Website

Personal academic website for **Carl A. Ziegler, Ph.D.**, exoplanet astronomer, Associate Professor of Astronomy, and Director of the SFA Observatory at Stephen F. Austin State University.

The site presents my research, teaching, scientific software, publications, observatory outreach, and professional background.

## Live Website

**[carlziegler.space](https://carlziegler.space/)**

## Research Areas

My work focuses on:

- Exoplanet detection and characterization
- Planet formation and evolution in multiple-star systems
- High-resolution imaging and speckle interferometry
- Adaptive optics and robotic astronomical instrumentation
- TESS and Kepler follow-up observations
- Long-period transiting planets
- Scientific computing and automated data-analysis pipelines

Featured projects include the **SOAR TESS Survey**, **Robo-AO Kepler Survey**, **Robo-SOAR**, and **One-Hit Wonders**.

## Gravitas

The website also features **[Gravitas](http://gravitas-sim.online/)**, an open-source, browser-based astronomy simulation developed for students and the public.

Gravitas allows users to build and explore physical systems involving:

- Orbital dynamics
- Black holes and compact-object mergers
- Gravitational waves and sonification
- Energy conservation
- Spacetime visualization
- Exoplanet transit light curves

Source code and documentation are available in the [Gravitas GitHub repository](https://github.com/gravitas-sim/gravitas-sim.github.io).

Outreach activities include:

- Regional school visits
- Scout and youth astronomy events

- Summer camps and community observing

Learn more at the [SFA Observatory website](https://www.sfasu.edu/academics/colleges/sciences-math/physics-engineering-astronomy/facilities-research/observatory).
