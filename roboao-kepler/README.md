# Robo-AO Kepler Survey microsite

This folder powers `https://carlziegler.space/roboao-kepler/`.

The page is a static, dependency-free explainer for the five-paper Robo-AO Kepler Survey. Its science numbers are drawn from the published survey series linked directly on the page. Interactive adaptive-optics and transit-dilution graphics are explanatory simulations rather than reconstructed observations.

External visual sources are credited in-page:

- Robo-AO at Palomar: Caltech / Christoph Baranec, University of Hawaiʻi.
- Kepler-field backdrop: NASA Goddard / Axel Mellinger, Central Michigan University.

The core survey team shown on the page is the intersection of the author lists of all five survey papers: Carl Ziegler, Nicholas M. Law, Christoph Baranec, Tim Morton, and Reed Riddle.
